KALSNET-ARTIFICIAL-GENERAL-INTELLIGENCE(NARROW-AGI)-SUITE

The AGI Mission Intelligence Suite, developed by Randy Singh of Kalsnet (KNet) Consulting Group, shows how general intelligence could work across very different problems. In each one it runs the same loop: observe the data, understand the goal, reason across sources, plan options, act only with human approval, verify the results, and learn. Every conclusion comes from a documented formula and is recorded in a step-by-step reasoning trace, so it's easy to see why the app recommended what it did. It is a transparent demonstration of narrow AGI applied to six focused domains. - Healthcare: reviews patient records and labs, ranks patients by risk, and drafts physician questions, care plans and appointments. - Cybersecurity: scores security alerts, maps the most likely attack path, and runs response playbooks once an analyst approves. - Software Development: turns a plain-language requirement into an architecture, code scaffold, build plan and test-and-fix cycle. - Government and DoD: fuses mission risks and simulates courses of action to recommend the best option to the decision authority. - Knowledge Graph: traces what a compromised device could reach and finds the single best link to break. - Everyday Travel: plans a two-week India trip with budget and currency, and replans the day when the family says "we're tired."
